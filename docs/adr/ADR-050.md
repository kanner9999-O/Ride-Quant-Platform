## Decision question

Does Chapter 10 §10.3.1's `compatibility_commitment` declaration — and the backward/forward
compatibility-classification rules that attach to it — apply to `causal_state_dependency_declaration`
specifically? Or is this one field categorically outside that commitment's scope, with its own
compatibility classified instead under Chapter 10 §10.3's general consumer-invalidation test (read
together with §10.7's downstream-impact-assessment requirement)?

## Smallest coherent decision surface

This ADR decides only the scope-boundary question for **`causal_state_dependency_declaration`**,
and, by the identical, closed test stated in the Decision below, any future field introduced under
`ADR-048`'s own `declared-state-dependencies`/`per_effect_event_contract` governance mechanism
specifically. It does **not** decide, and leaves entirely to `ADR-039`'s own existing, unchanged
authority or to later, separately-governed work:

- **`event_class`, `allowed_streams`, `merge_constraints`** — remain governed by Chapter 10
  §10.3/§10.3.1 against the contract's `compatibility_commitment`, exactly as `ADR-039` already
  fixed. Not reopened, not narrowed, not redefined.
- **Question (a)** — the platform-wide format-specific reader/schema policy `ADR-037`/`ADR-038`
  already named as missing. This ADR neither selects, sketches, nor requires one.
- Any specific contract's own `compatibility_commitment` **value** (`ADR-038`'s `backward_only` for
  `feature-computed`/`feature-fact-invalidated` is unchanged, unreopened, unamended).
- `ADR-048`'s own grammar for `causal_state_dependency_declaration` (unchanged).
- Whether `causal_state_dependency_declaration` specifically is ultimately classified
  breaking/non-breaking for Feature's own `v1.0 → v1.1` delta — this ADR fixes the **rule** that
  governs that classification; applying it to produce a concrete conclusion for Feature's own
  candidates remains separate, later work (see Consequences).
- Any Candle/Structure/Regime Event Contract's own content.

## Why (a) is not required to resolve (b)

(a) answers a question about DATA: given a declared compatibility direction, how permissively or
strictly does an old/new reader parse event-instance payload bytes it does not fully recognize. (b)
answers a logically prior, orthogonal question for this one field: whether
`causal_state_dependency_declaration` is "data" (something any payload-reading consumer ever
encounters) at all. It is not — confirmed true by direct inspection (Context, above) — and,
independently, its only defined consumer is gated by that consumer's own separate
`declared-state-dependencies` opt-in (Context, above), never by format/reader-tolerance behavior at
all. Resolving (b) for this field therefore requires only Chapter 8/Chapter 10's own already-locked
structural vocabulary (envelope vs. payload, the per-effect-declaration opt-in mechanism `ADR-048`
itself defines) — never a format-specific parsing-tolerance policy.

This reasoning is deliberately **not** extended to `event_class`/`allowed_streams`/
`merge_constraints`: unlike `causal_state_dependency_declaration`, every consumer must
unconditionally interpret those three regardless of its own configuration, and `ADR-039` already,
explicitly, ties their own compatibility classification to the compatibility-commitment axis — a
rule this ADR does not revisit, weaken, or treat as analogous to this field's own opt-in-gated
case.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Categorical exemption, generalized to every top-level sibling field of `payload_shape`
   (`event_class`, `allowed_streams`, `merge_constraints`, `causal_state_dependency_declaration`
   alike).** Rejected (this was v0.1's own Decision, corrected here per `ADR050-A-MAJ-01`) —
   directly contradicts `ADR-039`'s own explicit, unqualified text tying `event_class`/
   `allowed_streams`/`merge_constraints` changes to Chapter 10 §10.3/§10.3.1 compatibility-commitment
   classification. A decision cannot simultaneously claim `ADR-039` is unchanged and assert a rule
   that produces the opposite classification outcome for three of the four fields `ADR-039` itself
   already classifies.
2. **(CHOSEN) Narrow exemption for `causal_state_dependency_declaration` only (and, by the same
   closed test, any future `ADR-048`-mechanism field), grounded in its opt-in-gated consumption —
   not a general "non-`payload_shape` field" category.** Resolves (b) for the one field actually in
   question, using only already-locked structural vocabulary and `ADR-048`'s own opt-in consumption
   mechanism (Context, above) — never a reader/format-tolerance assumption. Leaves `ADR-039`'s own
   explicit rule for `event_class`/`allowed_streams`/`merge_constraints` completely untouched, so no
   contradiction with "ADR-039 is unchanged" can arise. Generalizes correctly to the one case that
   actually recurs — any future field `ADR-048`'s own specific governance mechanism introduces,
   which shares the identical opt-in-gated-consumption property by construction — without
   overreaching into fields that mechanism does not define.
3. **No exemption at all — `causal_state_dependency_declaration` is subject to the same §10.3.1
   data-presence rules as payload.** Rejected. Would import §10.3.1's "forward compatibility
   structurally unavailable because the data is absent" reasoning onto a field that is never
   present in any event's own data in the first place — a category error, not a stricter reading.
   Would also make Chapter 8 §8.2.3's `declared-state-dependencies` mechanism permanently unusable
   for any already-Published Event Contract platform-wide until the large, currently-unscoped
   reader/format-policy work (a) completes — a disproportionate, cascading blocker for a mechanism
   that touches no payload data at all.
4. **Merge (b) into (a) — resolve both together via one platform-wide reader/format policy now.**
   Rejected: (a) and (b) are orthogonal axes — (a) concerns reader tolerance for payload bytes; (b)
   concerns whether this field is payload at all. Resolving (a) first would not even answer (b),
   since (a)'s own machinery (JSON Schema/Avro/Protobuf-style unknown-field tolerance rules) has no
   defined meaning for a field that is never serialized. Attempting it now would also be a far
   larger decision than this bounded question requires, contrary to `G-ADR-002`'s "khó đảo ngược,
   không phải chi phí thấp" scoping discipline.

## Decision

**Chapter 10 §10.3.1's `compatibility_commitment` declaration, and the backward/forward
compatibility-classification rules that attach to it, do NOT apply to `causal_state_dependency_declaration`.**
This field — defined by Approved `ADR-048` as a top-level sibling of `payload_shape` on the Event
Contract version-artifact, never nested inside `payload_shape`/`payload_semantics_and_invariants`,
never serialized into any event instance's own payload bytes, and consumed only by a validator
acting on a consumer's own prior, separately-declared `causal_closure_policy.mode:
declared-state-dependencies` opt-in (Chapter 8 §8.2.3) — is categorically **outside**
`compatibility_commitment`'s scope. This exemption applies to `causal_state_dependency_declaration`
specifically, and, by the identical test (top-level sibling field defined by `ADR-048`'s own
`declared-state-dependencies`/`per_effect_event_contract` governance mechanism, opt-in-consumed,
never serialized), to any future field that same mechanism introduces — never to any other field.

**`event_class`, `allowed_streams`, and `merge_constraints` are explicitly, affirmatively
EXCLUDED from this exemption.** `ADR-039`'s own existing rule — that changes to these three fields
(together with payload shape) are classified breaking/non-breaking under Chapter 10 §10.3/§10.3.1
against the contract's declared `compatibility_commitment` — remains fully controlling, unchanged,
unreopened, un-reinterpreted by this ADR in any way.

**Compatibility classification for `causal_state_dependency_declaration` instead uses Chapter 10
§10.3's own general breaking-change test, read together with §10.7's downstream-impact-assessment
requirement** — a currently-valid, actually-opted-in consumer becomes invalid, or does not,
evaluated against the contract's real, registered consumer/pinning topology
(`context-map.yaml`/`module-registry.yaml`/the consumer's own Input Contract, the same evidentiary
standard `ADR-038`/`ADR-047` already use) — never against §10.3.1's data-presence rules, and never
by assuming any reader-tolerance behavior.

**Unaffected, not reopened:**

- A new `contract_version` is still always required for this field's first addition to an
  already-`Published` artifact (`ADR-039`'s immutability model, unaffected).
- `event_class`/`allowed_streams`/`merge_constraints`/`payload_shape`/`payload_semantics_and_invariants`
  deltas remain governed by Chapter 10 §10.3/§10.3.1 exactly as `ADR-039` already fixed —
  `feature.md`'s own already-closed `BREAKING` classification of `ADR-037`'s
  `computation_dependency_content_evidence` (a true `payload_shape` field) is not reopened,
  reclassified, or affected by this Decision in any way.
- Any contract's own declared `compatibility_commitment` value (`backward_only`, etc.) is unchanged.
- Question (a) — the platform-wide reader/format policy — remains exactly as open, and exactly as
  separately governed, as `ADR-037`/`ADR-038` already state.

## Consequences

This ADR resolves question (b) only, and only for `causal_state_dependency_declaration`. It does
not itself classify Feature's own delta as breaking or non-breaking — that classification is a
separate, later, bounded application of this Decision's rule (no Feature `v1.1` file is touched
here). Applying this Decision's rule to that concrete case (non-normative preview, not asserted as
this ADR's own conclusion): `causal_state_dependency_declaration` is a top-level sibling of
`payload_shape` on both Feature `v1.1` candidates (confirmed, Context above), never serialized into
any event instance's own payload; under this Decision's rule, its compatibility classification
would therefore use Chapter 10 §10.3's general test (read with §10.7) rather than §10.3.1 — and
because `context-aggregator` is not currently relying on anything this field would remove, narrow,
or reinterpret, and in fact cannot satisfy its own already-declared `declared-state-dependencies`
mode for `feature-engine-feature` until a version carrying this field exists, no currently-valid,
actually-opted-in consumer would become invalid. A follow-on, separately-governed bounded
transaction should: (1) apply this rule concretely to close that classification for Feature's own
delta, citing this ADR as authority; (2) correct
`docs/project/feature-causal-state-dependency-version-impact-001.md`'s own stale §3.3 claim (that no
registered consumer uses `declared-state-dependencies` mode for Feature's stream) against
`context-market-input.yaml`'s actual content; (3) when any Candle/Structure/Regime Event Contract
first needs its own `causal_state_dependency_declaration` addition, cite this ADR directly rather
than re-litigating the scope question. This ADR does not change `ADR-037`/`ADR-038`/`ADR-039`/
`ADR-048`'s own decisions, does not modify any Constitution chapter, does not modify
`context-market-input.yaml` or any other Input Contract, and does not authorize publication of any
Event Contract. `event_class`/`allowed_streams`/`merge_constraints` compatibility classification is
unaffected in every respect.

## Non-retroactivity

This Decision does not reopen, reclassify, or retroactively alter `feature.md` v0.6's own
already-closed `BREAKING` classification of `ADR-037`'s `computation_dependency_content_evidence`
(a genuine `payload_shape` field, correctly governed by §10.3.1 both before and after this ADR). It
does not reopen, reclassify, or retroactively alter any prior or future classification of
`event_class`/`allowed_streams`/`merge_constraints` changes, which `ADR-039` already governs and
this ADR does not touch. It applies prospectively, and only, to the compatibility-classification
scope question for `causal_state_dependency_declaration` (and its own governance mechanism's future
fields) on any Event Contract version-artifact, current or future.
